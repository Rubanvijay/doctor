What is homeopathic treatment?

Homeopathy is a natural system of medicine that uses highly diluted substances to trigger the body's own healing mechanisms. It treats the whole person, not just the symptoms.

Are homeopathic medicines safe?

Yes, homeopathic medicines are extremely safe with no side effects. They are non-toxic, non-addictive and can be safely taken by people of all ages, including children and pregnant women.

What conditions can homeopathy treat?

Homeopathy can effectively treat chronic diseases, skin conditions, respiratory issues, digestive disorders, women's health problems, children's health issues, and various acute conditions.

How long does treatment take?

The duration varies depending on the condition. Acute conditions may improve quickly, while chronic conditions may require longer treatment. Dr. Durai will provide a personalized treatment plan.

Do I need to stop my current medications?
